# EU AI Act Article 16 — High-Risk AI Provider Compliance Program

A practical compliance program built around Article 16 of the EU AI Act, which sets out twelve legal obligations for any organization that **provides** a high-risk AI system. This project turns those obligations into something operational: a lifecycle workflow, a provider checklist mapped to practical actions, a RACI matrix, an evidence register, and a non-compliance risk table.

**Case study:** CreditScope AI, a fictional company providing an AI-based creditworthiness assessment system to banks — classified high-risk under Annex III of the EU AI Act.

📄 **[EU-AI-Act-Article-16-Provider-Compliance-Program.md](EU-AI-Act-Article-16-Provider-Compliance-Program.md)** — the full program: the official Article 16 text, a Mermaid lifecycle diagram separating pre-market from post-market obligations, a 12-row obligation checklist, a RACI matrix, an evidence register, a non-compliance risk table, and a final governance recommendation.

## At a Glance

Article 16's twelve obligations split cleanly into two phases:

| Phase | Obligations |
|---|---|
| **Pre-Market** (one-time gate) | (a) Section 2 compliance · (b) Provider identification · (c) Quality management system · (d) Technical documentation · (e) Log storage · (f) Conformity assessment · (g) EU Declaration of Conformity · (h) CE marking · (i) Registration |
| **Post-Market** (continuous) | (j) Corrective actions · (k) Demonstrate conformity on request · (l) Accessibility |

The most common compliance mistake this project highlights: treating Article 16 as a one-time launch checklist. Nine of the twelve obligations are pre-market gates — but three of them never close.
